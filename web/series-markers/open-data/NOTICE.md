This directory is the public, machine-readable derivative of the selected SkipDB records used by ARKHEN. Download `skipdb.json` for the source selection, transformed edition records, provenance and coverage. Per-series manifests are available at `<series-slug>/manifest.json`.

Exports are refreshed before the corresponding Supabase records are updated. They contain no user identities, favorites, playback credentials or submitter identifiers. Community timestamps remain unverified for a particular WPLAY release. Titles and aliases in the series registry identify works and are not SkipDB database contents.
